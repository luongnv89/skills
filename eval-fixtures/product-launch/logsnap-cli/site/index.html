Noisy logs, one screen.

logsnap groups thousands of log lines into templates, so you see the 12 things that happened instead of 40,000 lines.

pip install logsnap
logsnap app.log

What it does

  • Clusters similar lines: numbers, IDs and timestamps are masked automatically.
  • Baselines: save a snapshot, then see only what is new after a deploy.
  • Works on files and stdin (kubectl, docker, journalctl).
  • Local only: your logs never leave your machine.
