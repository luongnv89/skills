# Lantern Reef: App Store listing (en-US)

Name: Lantern Reef
Subtitle: Light the deep, one reef at a time

Description:
Swim a lantern fish through nine dark reefs and light the coral as you go.
Every reef takes about two minutes, so a dive fits any break.
Collect glowing creatures for your log, and watch out for stingrays.
The Shallows are free. Deep Pass opens the Twilight and Midnight seas.
No ads. No tracking.
