# Lantern Reef

A landscape diving game for iPhone and iPad (Flutter). Swim a lantern fish
through dark reefs, light the coral and collect the glowing creatures before
your oxygen runs out.

## Features

- **Nine reefs** in three seas. The Shallows (three reefs) are free; the Deep
  Pass in-app purchase opens the Twilight and Midnight seas (six reefs).
- **Collect creatures**: touch a glowing creature to add it to your log.
  Stingrays sting instead: they cost oxygen and are never collected.
- **One-thumb controls**: drag anywhere to swim; tap to flash the lantern.
- **No ads, no tracking.**
