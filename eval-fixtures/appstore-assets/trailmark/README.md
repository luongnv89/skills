# Trailmark

A hiking journal for iPhone and iPad. Log every trail you walk, see your
weekly distance and elevation, and keep your favorite routes one tap away.

## Features

- **Trail log** — every hike with distance, duration and elevation gain.
- **Live hike** — record your route with a running timer and pace.
- **Stats** — weekly distance chart and lifetime totals.
- **Favorites** — star the trails you want to walk again.
- **iPad split view** — browse the log and the trail details side by side.
- **Private by design** — hikes are stored on your device only.
