# Trailmark design rules

Rules for Trailmark's marketing images. Follow them unless a request says otherwise.

1. Backgrounds are one flat color, #F4F1EA, never a gradient or glow.
2. Accent words in headlines use #1F6E4B.
3. No uppercase or letter-spaced text.
4. No drop shadows: separate a device from the background with a 1px #D8D2C4 outline.
5. No floating callout cards.
