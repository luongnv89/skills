# Design rules

Rules for the tally brand. Follow them unless a request says otherwise.

1. Square corners: every shape has a corner radius of 0.
2. Colors: ink `#1F2937` and one accent `#D97706`. No other color, except white or black in single-color variants.
3. Marks are filled shapes. No strokes.
4. The wordmark is lowercase, set in IBM Plex Sans at weight 600.
5. No gradients.
6. Showcase cards may use a soft box-shadow.
