# PRD: ShiftSwap

Source: idea.md, validate.md

## 1. Product Overview
Shift-swap requests with manager approval for small retail shops.

## 2. User Personas
Maya, store manager; Leo, part-time cashier.

## 3. Feature Requirements
- Must: post a shift for swap
- Must: claim a posted shift
- Must: manager approves or rejects a swap
- Should: SMS notification on approval
- Could: weekly schedule export

## 9. Open Questions & Risks
- Adoption by managers who already use paper schedules.
