Plug in. Power up.

In The Matrix, Tank loads a program, Neo opens his eyes, and says “I know kung fu.” The mark captures that moment. A skill card is seated inside the agent, and together they form the universal power-on symbol. One card goes in and the agent is instantly capable.

The cardA memory card with a chamfered corner and four contacts. Each skill is a self-contained module you slot in.
The ringThe agent. It is an open loop with a single port, and the card passes through it.
The greenGreen only appears on the card. It is the “powered on” LED, and it nods to the Matrix phosphor.
