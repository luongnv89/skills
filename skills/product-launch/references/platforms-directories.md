# Directories, review sites, AI directories and profiles

Read only by the Step 3 live-check workers and the Step 5 writers that own these
platforms. Facts come from each platform's own pages, read on 2026-10-22, unless the line
says otherwise. Every entry here is paste-ready.

These are slow, evergreen listings. They are not launch-day traffic: they bring search
visibility, backlinks and credibility over weeks, so the plan schedules them as a
**directory drip** after L-day.

## alternativeto — AlternativeTo

Submit: "Suggest new application" in the user menu (needs a verified email) · Confidence:
high

- **Fits:** software on any platform, commercial or open source. Discovery runs through
  "alternatives to X" pages.
- **Excluded:**
  - Non-software (blogs, guides, coupon sites); single-function online tools.
  - "thin wrappers around someone else's AI model".
  - Services; apps limited to one city or country; clones.
- **Cost:** free. A priority review fee is shown at checkout. It buys speed, not approval;
  a third-party source says $5 (unverified).
- **Free wait:** "several thousand apps" in the queue, with no date. Priority review takes
  1–2 business days.
- **Fields:**
  - Platforms; license; descriptions; tags.
  - Official and creator URLs and store links, in the URL fields only. The description
    must not hold links, emails or phone numbers.
  - Icon: square PNG or SVG, 280×280 or larger, with a transparent background.
  - Screenshots.
- **Red lines:** incentivized likes, fake accounts, spam lists, UTM tags on the official
  URL.
- **Link:** unknown.
- **Do:** get the product suggested as an alternative on the pages of the incumbents it
  replaces.
- **Sources:** alternativeto.net/faq and /about/terms.

## saashub — SaaSHub

Submit: https://www.saashub.com/services/submit · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** SaaS, most software, OSS and self-hosted products, mobile apps with decent
  sites.
- **Excluded:**
  - Waitlist pages and unreleased products ("rejected immediately").
  - Free subdomains; non-English products; agencies.
- **Cost:** free. Featured Listing costs $99/month.
- **Free wait:** not published. Submissions without competitors listed go "to the bottom
  of the queue", and verified products get priority.
- **Fields:**
  - Product URL; a few categories.
  - Competitors ("strongly encouraged"): these decide which alternatives pages show
    the product.
- **Verification:** an email on the product's domain, or an HTML snippet. Re-verify every
  3 months.
- **Link:** dofollow only for verified listings (observed).
- **Sources:** saashub.com/services/submit, /faq and /site/product_verification.

## g2 — G2

Submit: https://www.g2.com/products/new, then claim the listing · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** "G2 only lists business-to-business (B2B) software and services". G2 judges
  the category fit from your website.
- **Excluded:** B2C; "products that are currently in the alpha or beta stage"; duplicates;
  integrations; no registered business entity.
- **Cost:**
  - Free basic profile, plus the 'Users Love Us' badge at 20 reviews averaging 4.0 or more.
  - Starter: $299/month in year 1, then $599/month.
- **Timing:** about 3–5 business days of verification, then 1–3 business days for the
  claim.
- **Mechanics:** reviews drive Grid placement. This is a long-term review program, not a
  launch.
- **Red lines:**
  - Collecting only positive reviews, or making incentives conditional on positive
    reviews.
  - Incentives over $100.
  - Reviews from your own or a competitor's employees.
- **Link:** unknown.
- **Sources:** sell.g2.com/plans and /create-a-profile, legal.g2.com/community-guidelines,
  and research.g2.com/methodology.

## capterra — Capterra (also covers GetApp and Software Advice)

Submit: https://www.g2.com/products/new. G2 bought Gartner Digital Markets (announced
2026-01-29), so one profile appears on all three sites · Confidence: medium (read from
archived copies of Capterra's own pages)

- **Fits:** packaged business software that is "publicly available, with a call to
  action". A beta needs a working site, a public offer, and at least one review in its
  first year.
- **Excluded:** a waitlist-only CTA; personal-productivity apps; marketplaces; clones;
  bespoke software.
- **Cost:** a free profile that "does not generate conversions, clicks, or leads" (no
  click-out). Paid PPC needs approval; prices are not public.
- **Fields:** vendor name and logo; product name; a unique description; real-UI
  screenshots; an optional English video (YouTube, Vimeo or Wistia).
- **Description rules:** third person; no superlatives, CTAs or contact details; no line
  breaks, symbols or HTML; no "Inc" or "LLC". The content team may rewrite it.
- **Link:** none on the free profile.
- **Sources:** capterra.com/vendors (Wayback, 2026-09-21), g2digitalmarkets.com/profiles,
  and getapp.com/listing_guidelines (Wayback, 2026-04-18).

## trustradius — TrustRadius (HG Customer Voice)

Claim: https://solutions.trustradius.com/claim-your-profile/ · Confidence: medium

- **Fits:** B2B technology for mid-market and enterprise buyers. There is no public form
  for adding a product that is not listed yet.
- **Cost:** a free profile; Premium pricing only through a demo.
- **Mechanics:** long, verified reviews feed the trScore and awards. Top Rated needs 10+
  reviews in 12 months and a trScore of 7.5 or more.
- **Red lines:** incentives that depend on sentiment; employee or competitor reviews;
  inviting only known advocates (flagged as selection bias).
- **Sources:** trustradius.com/vendors, solutions.trustradius.com/claim-your-profile/,
  and /awards/.

## sourceforge — SourceForge

Submit: open source at https://sourceforge.net/p/import_project/github/ (import from
GitHub); business listing at https://sourceforge.net/software/vendors/new · Confidence:
medium

- **Fits:**
  - Hosting: open source with an OSD-compliant license.
  - Directory: commercial software. The same form feeds Slashdot's software directory.
- **Setup:** an account, plus phone verification for open-source projects.
- **Cost:** OSS hosting is free (unverified). It is unstated whether a basic business
  listing is free; paid tiers are priced by budget.
- **Timing:** an OSS project is created immediately.
- **Fields:**
  - OSS: project name, and a URL name of 3–30 letters, digits or dashes.
  - Business form: company details, title, square logo, full-functionality description,
    audience.
- **Do:** after importing, enable Files, then "GitHub Integration", so releases sync.
- **Sources:** sourceforge.net/software/vendors/new and /pricing, and
  /p/forge/documentation (GitHub Importer).

## sideprojectors — SideProjectors

Submit: https://www.sideprojectors.com/submit (choose sell, co-founder, or **showcase**) ·
Confidence: medium

- **Fits:** side projects to sell, or to showcase for feedback.
- **Excluded:** non-English listings, poor detail or images, exaggerated metrics ("Don't
  exaggerate - we check").
- **Cost:** free, with no commission. Priority review is priced at submission. ID
  verification costs $5.
- **Timing:** 24 h to 5 business days.
- **Fields:**
  - name ≤50; one-sentence pitch ≤80; homepage; description.
  - up to 5 markets; logo ≥256×256; screenshots; tech stack; revenue or "pre-revenue".
  - An opt-in at the end cross-posts the project to ComingUp.
- **Link:** unknown.
- **Sources:** sideprojectors.com help pages (help/submit, help/approval-process).

## startup-fame — Startup Fame

Submit: https://startupfa.me/login ("Submit your startup") · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** startups and serious side projects. The site needs an English version and a
  domain rating above 0.
- **Cost:**
  - Free "Verified" tier: requires a visible dofollow badge linking to startupfa.me,
    checked by a bot. A missing badge deactivates the listing.
  - Highlight: $19/month.
- **Timing:** free review takes "several business days"; paid listings publish
  instantly.
- **Fields:** a URL, from which its AI fills the profile.
- **Link:** dofollow.
- **Sources:** startupfa.me/pricing, /about and /guides/homepage-badge.

## crunchbase — Crunchbase

Create: Resources, then Create Profile (after a free account) · Mode: paste-ready, third
person · Confidence: medium

- **Fits:** any company. It is a credibility profile for investors, press and sales, not
  a traffic source.
- **Setup:**
  - A free account with LinkedIn or Google social authentication.
  - To edit locked fields, verify with a company-domain email.
  - Search first to avoid creating a duplicate.
- **Fields:**
  - Logo; founded date; website and socials.
  - Short and long description in the third person, with no "best" and no "new". Start
    the long description with a one-sentence summary.
  - Headquarters; 3–5 industries; founders; closed funding rounds with a press link.
- **Sources:** support.crunchbase.com articles "How do I create a Crunchbase profile",
  "Edit a Profile" and "best practices".

## wellfound — Wellfound

Mode: plan it **only if the profile says the company is hiring** · Confidence: medium

- **Fits:** startups that are hiring. Wellfound is now a recruiting platform. Its code of
  conduct restricts anyone "advertising or soliciting goods or services", so it is never
  a product-promotion channel.
- **Setup:** a personal user profile; claim an existing company profile rather than
  creating a duplicate.
- **Cost:** job posts are free (with salary ranges); job ads start at $200.
- **Sources:** wellfound.com/recruit/pricing, and help.wellfound.com articles 708
  (code of conduct) and 720.

## twelve-tools — Twelve Tools

- **Excluded:** adult, gambling, drug-related or low-quality sites.
- **Cost:**

## theresanaiforthat — There's An AI For That

Submit: https://theresanaiforthat.com/launch/ · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** "AI tools, MCPs, GPTs and AI influencers (non-human)". Non-AI products are
  excluded.
- **Cost:**
  - No free self-serve tier. The only free route is a monthly X thread, where one tool
    per thread is picked.
  - Low traffic: $49 one-time (permanent listing, $100 PPC bonus).
  - Maximum Exposure: $437 (newsletter slot to 2.5M+, $300 PPC bonus). It was $347 in
    August.
- **Timing:** reviewed in 1–2 days; the launch date can be scheduled. A full refund if
  the tool is not published.
- **Sequencing rule:** the PPC bonus requires that "Your tool must not appear on any other
  platform before the launch". If TAAFT is in the plan, schedule it **before** every other
  AI directory.
- **Fields:** a tool URL that autofills the page, editable after email verification;
  package; launch date and timezone.
- **Sources:** theresanaiforthat.com/launch/.

## future-tools — Future Tools

Submit: https://www.futuretools.io/submit-a-tool (no account) · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** publicly available AI software tools, curated by Matt Wolfe.
- **Excluded:** custom GPTs; waitlist or not-yet-public products; concepts already
  listed several times; low effort or poor UX; adult content; non-tools.
  - More than 75% of submissions are rejected.
- **Cost:** free. Featured listings and newsletter slots are priced on request.
- **Timing:** no SLA. Resubmit only if the tool is not listed within a week.
- **Fields:**
  - Tool name, URL, short description.
  - Category: Chat, Generative Art, Generative Video, Generative Code, Text-To-Speech,
    Search Engines, Productivity, Marketing, Design, Writing, Music, Research,
    Education, Finance, Healthcare, Legal, Sales, Customer Support or Other.
  - Pricing: Free, Freemium, Paid or Open Source.
  - Email.
- **Sources:** futuretools.io/submit-a-tool, /faq and /advertise.

## ai-directories — AI Directories (aidirectori.es)

Submit: https://aidirectori.es/submit-ai-tool · Confidence: high

- **What it is:** mainly a paid done-for-you service that submits a tool to 30–100+
  directories ($99, $149 or $199). It also has its own catalog, and a free list of
  320+ directories you can submit to yourself.
- **Catalog cost:** free with a backlink (listed within 72 h, dofollow), or $49 one-time

- **Do:** treat the paid submission service as optional. Launch Llama, Shipybara,

- **Sources:** aidirectori.es, /submit-ai-tool and /pricing.

## launch-llama — Launch Llama tools

- **Fits:** AI tools, dev tools, indie startups and SaaS. Despite the branding, it is not
  AI-only.
- **Cost:**
  - Free: review in about 4 weeks. You must upvote 5 tools, leave one rating with

  - Instant Launch: $39 (skips the wait, the upvote step and the badge step; dofollow).

  - A free link is dofollow while the badge is found, or once the listing has 50
    upvotes.
  - Paid listings are dofollow.
- **Sources:** tools.launchllama.co, /pricing and /llms.txt.
