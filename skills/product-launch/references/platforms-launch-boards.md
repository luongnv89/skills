# Launch boards: daily, weekly and monthly launch sites

Read only by the Step 3 live-check workers and the Step 5 writers that own these
platforms. Facts come from each platform's own pages, read on 2026-10-22, unless the line
says otherwise. Traffic, DR and subscriber numbers are the platforms' own claims. Every
board here is paste-ready.

Contents: what-launched-today · betalist · uneed · peerlist · devhunt · launching-next ·
microlaunch · betterlaunch · foundrlist · peerpush · startupbase · aura-plus-plus ·
shipybara · saascity · pitchwall · scrolllaunch · discoverlaunches · rank-in-public · tinylaunch ·
tiny-startups · nick-launches

**Common pattern: badge-gated.** Many free tiers give a dofollow link, or a free slot,
only while the platform's badge links back from your homepage. The badge usually links
to your listing page, which exists only after you submit, so the founder adds it after
creating the listing and keeps it live. Removing the badge can delist the product or
turn the link back to nofollow.

## what-launched-today — What Launched Today

Submit: https://whatlaunched.today/dashboard/submit (needs an account) · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** any tech product, existing ones included. Not spam, misleading or
  inappropriate content.
- **Cost:**
  - Free: lifetime dofollow link, standard queue.
  - Premium Launch: 27 € (≈$29). Launches at once, skips the queue, and stays on the
    homepage for several days.
  - Featured Spot: from 36 € for 2 weeks. Sidebar, newsletter and DR70+ link.
- **Free wait:** about 3 months. There are 5 free slots a day, and the nearest 5 days are
  paid-only.
- **Cadence:** daily, rolling over at 00:00 UTC, with weekly and monthly leaderboards
  ranked by votes.
- **Fields:** name, URL, tagline, rich-text description, tags, category, logo and launch
  date. No limits are published.
- **Warning:** the public homepage HTML exposed submitter emails for featured listings
  (2026-10-22). Submit from an alias address.
- **Sources:** whatlaunched.today, /llms.txt and /terms.

## betalist — BetaList

Submit: https://betalist.com/submit (needs sign-in) · Confidence: medium (read from
archive snapshots dated 2026-07 to 2026-09)

- **Fits:** pre-launch or just-launched tech startups with a clear value proposition, an
  original design, their own domain, and a sign-up or access CTA.
- **Excluded:**
  - Products launched "weeks ago or longer", or with significant press already.
  - Templates and default Bootstrap pages; LaunchRock, Unbounce or KickoffLabs pages.
  - Free subdomains (vercel.app, netlify.app, herokuapp.com).
  - App Store, Google Play, Kickstarter or Facebook links in place of a site.
  - "Follow us on X" as the only CTA.
  - Blogs, courses, books, newsletters, agencies.
- **Cost:** paid only ("All submissions are paid"), with a full automatic refund if the
  product is not selected. Plan prices appear only after sign-in (unverified). Boost,
  after you are featured, costs $99/week.
- **Timing:** depends on the plan. Each startup is featured once pre-launch and once at
  launch, a few weeks apart.
- **Link:** dofollow ("Visit Site" goes through a 301 redirect).
- **Sources:** betalist.com/support, /criteria and /boost (Wayback copies).

## uneed — Uneed

Submit: https://uneed.best/submit-a-tool (no account needed to start) · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** SaaS, dev tools, AI products "with a clear use case", mobile apps, extensions,
  hardware and marketplaces.
- **Excluded:**
  - Generic AI wrappers, copycats, products "mainly seeking a backlink".
  - Closed products: waitlist, invite or sales-call gated.
  - NSFW products. On the free tier only, oversaturated categories.
- **Cost:**
  - Free: joins the line.
  - Fast-track: $14.99, about 14 days out.
  - Skip the line: $39.99, any date.
  - Pro: $99/year.
- **Free wait:** at least 30 days, and possibly months.
- **Cadence:** daily at 12:00 AM PST.
  - A free launch must score 10 to stay published and 20 to keep its dofollow link.
  - Votes are weighted, and upvoting your own product is explicitly allowed.
  - The top 3 earn badges.
- **Fields:** a name and URL first. Uneed scrapes the page to prefill the rest.
- **Red lines:** votes from account batches or a single IP are removed, and repeat
  offenders are banned.
- **Link:** dofollow (DR 75 domain) with the score conditions above. Paid launches keep
  it for life.
- **Do:** launch Tuesday to Thursday (Uneed's own guide), and line up supporters to
  reach a score of 20.
- **Sources:** uneed.best/pricing, /how-it-works, /launch-guide and /faqs.

## peerlist — Peerlist Launchpad

Submit: https://peerlist.io/launchpad (the "Launch" button, picking a project from your
profile) · Confidence: medium

- **Fits:** coding projects (dev tools, extensions, libraries), design projects and
  digital products.
- **Excluded:** company or non-personal profiles, agencies launching for clients,
  products you don't own, and gambling or adult content.
- **Setup:**
  - A verified individual profile with a real name and photo. Work-email verification
    is free; ID verification has a small fee.
  - The project must already be on your profile at 100% completion.
- **Cost:** free.
- **Cadence:** weekly. The window opens every Monday and the launch runs the week.
  - The ranking blends upvotes, comments, views and link visits. Only qualitative
    comments count.
  - The order is random on Monday and Tuesday.
  - The top 3 appear in the weekly email.
- **Fields:** name, tagline, URL, up to 4 cover images, video URL, tech stack, category,
  open-source flag and description. No limits are published.
- **Red lines:** DMing strangers for upvotes, or asking for upvotes in others' comments
  (counted as spam). Plagiarism.
- **Link:** only the week's top 5 get a backlink.
- **Sources:** peerlist.io/launchpad, and help.peerlist.io (Launchpad guidelines and FAQ).

## devhunt — DevHunt

Submit: https://devhunt.org/login, then /account/tools/new · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** developer tools only: OSS, APIs, SDKs, frameworks, CLIs, testing,
  monitoring, DevOps, databases, auth, AI agents and MCP servers.
- **Cost:**
  - Free listing: a permanent page with a nofollow link.
  - Launch: $19. Pick the week, get a newsletter spot and a dofollow link.
  - Boost: $49, which wins ties.
  - Super boost: $99, 3 per week.
- **Cadence:** weekly, from Tuesday 00:00 UTC to Monday 23:59 UTC. Most votes at the end
  of the week wins. Voters sign in with GitHub or Google.
- **Fields:**
  - Logo; name (≥3 characters, unique); slogan (≥10 characters); website and GitHub URLs.
  - Description; pricing type; categories; demo video.
  - Screenshots: 3 to 5. The first one is the social preview.
  - These come from the public repo's form (medium confidence).
- **Link:** dofollow on paid launches.
- **Sources:** devhunt.org, /faq, and the github.com/MarsX-dev/devhunt form source.

## launching-next — Launching Next

Submit: https://www.launchingnext.com/submit/ (no account) · Confidence: medium

- **Fits:** startups and side projects, editorially published. There is no voting.
- **Cost:** free. A $99 upgrade buys consideration within 1 business day, but no
  guaranteed publication.
- **Timing:** reviewed daily. An email arrives only if the product is published.
- **Fields:**
  - Startup name ≤100; URL ≤100.
  - Headline: 5–8 words, ≤100 characters.
  - Description ≤2,500 characters.
  - 5–10 comma-separated tags.
  - Startup type; planned 90-day marketing spend; name and email.
  - There is no image upload.
- **Link:** mixed. Some listings are dofollow and some nofollow, with no stated policy.
- **Sources:** launchingnext.com and /submit/.

- **Fits:** startups and software, in Business and Consumer boards.

## betterlaunch — BetterLaunch

Submit: https://www.betterlaunch.co/submit (needs sign-in) · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** live software products. Not-live sites, link schemes and spam are declined.
- **Cost:**
  - Free: a Monday at least 45 days out, or 14 days sooner after you vote for 3 launches.
    Seven days in the feed. The link is nofollow, or dofollow with the badge.
  - Skip the queue: $9.
  - Starter: $19 (7 days pinned, permanent dofollow).
  - Pro: $49.
- **Cadence:** weekly batches on Monday at 00:00 UTC.
- **Fields:** paste a URL and the site drafts the name, tagline, description and
  categories. You can add screenshots. No limits are published.
- **Link:** badge-gated dofollow on free; dofollow on every paid plan.
- **Sources:** betterlaunch.co, /pricing and /how-it-works.

## foundrlist — FoundrList

Submit: https://foundrlist.com/agent ("Launch a Product") · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** a product you own or are authorized to represent.
- **Free qualifier:** add the FoundrList badge, or upvote 3 launches and comment on 1.
- **Cost:**
  - Free: about 4 weeks out, a week on the board, nofollow link.
  - Standard: $29 (live today, 7 days at the top, dofollow DR 73).
  - Featured: $49 for 14 days.
- **Cadence:** weekly. A new board opens every Monday at 12:01 AM PT. Paid launches show
  first, then the rest by upvotes and recency.
- **Fields:** paste a link and its AI agent drafts the listing for you to edit. No limits
  are published.
- **Red lines:** buying or trading votes, comments or reviews; bots; multiple or borrowed
  accounts. Removing a required badge can end the benefit it unlocked.
- **Link:** dofollow on paid plans only.
- **Sources:** foundrlist.com, /pricing, /legal/terms and /legal/editorial-policy.

## peerpush — PeerPush

Submit: https://peerpush.com/submit (peerpush.net redirects there) · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** software: web, mobile (App Store or Google Play URLs), API, desktop, MCP and
  CLI.
- **Cost:**
  - Free: waits behind paid launches, and is invisible until its slot comes.
  - Standard: $39 (live at once).
  - With promotion: $89 or $229.
  - 400 PeerPush points launch a product for free. Points come from engaging with other
    products, with a daily cap; points decay.
- **Cadence:** continuous. Day, Week and Month awards are set at midnight UTC.
- **Fields:** a URL, from which it drafts the listing. The API requires the name,
  tagline, description, URL, logo URL and contact email.
- **Ranking:** engagement, points, ratings, click-through, freshness, plus a "Verified"
  boost when your site links back to your PeerPush page.
- **Link:** dofollow (observed).
- **Sources:** peerpush.com, /pricing, /faq and /rules.

## startupbase — StartupBase

Submit: https://startupbase.io/submit (sign in with Google, LinkedIn or X) · Confidence:
high

- **Fits:** products with a working website and a real product.
- **Excluded:** placeholder sites, agencies with no product, resold software and key

## aura-plus-plus — Aura++

Submit: https://auraplusplus.com/projects/submit · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** real, working software and digital products.
- **Cost:**
  - Free: 5 slots a day, schedulable up to 365 days ahead.
  - Premium: $29 (skips the queue, dofollow DR 73, launch blog post).
  - Premium Plus: $69.
- **Timing:** review takes 24–48 h. Launches go live daily at 8:00 AM UTC, ranked by
  upvotes and comments. A relaunch needs a 30-day cooldown on the free tier.
- **Fields** (from its API):
  - name ≤120; website URL; makerName ≤120.
  - description **≥200 words** (its launch guide says 150–300 words).
  - logo and product image (HTTPS URLs).
  - 1–3 categories; 1–5 tech-stack items; platforms; pricing.
- **Link:** a free link is dofollow only with a top-3 finish **and** a verified badge.
  The badge snippet for the dark theme has a broken `https://https://` src, so fix it
  before pasting.
- **Sources:** auraplusplus.com/pricing, /faq, /launch-guide and /openapi.json.

## saascity — SaaSCity

conflicts across its own pages)

- **Fits:** SaaS, AI, dev tools and more, with one listing per domain.
- **Excluded:** waitlist pages; free-subdomain hosts (github.io, netlify.app and similar);

- **Cost:**
  - Free: badge required, 15 slots a week.

  - Premium: $39.99.
- **Free wait:** its pricing page showed "~336-day review queue", while its FAQ says "a

- **Cadence:** weekly rounds from Monday 00:00 UTC, ranked by points.
- **Fields:**
  - title 3–60; tagline 10–100; description 50–5,000, plain text.
  - website URL: HTTPS on your own domain.
  - One category; up to 10 tags; optional X and GitHub URLs.
- **Link:** dofollow, on the free tier once the badge is verified.
- **Do:** read its readiness checklist before submitting: HTTPS, no `noindex`, meta and

## discoverlaunches — DiscoverLaunches

Submit: https://discoverlaunches.com/submit (Google login) · Confidence: high

- **Fits:** SaaS, AI, dev tools and other software.
- **Cost:**
  - Free: 20 slots per ISO week; pick this week or one of the next five.
  - Dofollow: $9 one-time (permanent dofollow, no badge needed).

## tinylaunch — TinyLaunch

Submit: the "Submit Product" button on https://www.tinylaunch.com (email OTP login) ·
Confidence: high

- **Fits:** startups and products, in one category from a fixed list.
- **Cost:**
  - Free: about 4–5 weeks. On 2026-10-22 the next free Monday was 2026-11-23.
  - Premium: $39 (next Monday, dofollow).
- **Cadence:** weekly, launching Mondays, ranked by upvotes. The top 3 get badges.

## nick-launches — Nick Launches

Submit: https://nicklaunches.com/submit/ (needs an account) · Confidence: medium

- **Fits:** indie AI, SaaS, dev, marketing and productivity tools.
- **Excluded:** duplicate launches, unavailable sites, misleading claims.
- **Cost:**
  - Free: the earliest week with room. The badge must stay visible to remain listed.
  - Paid (from its own 2026-08-09 post): $19, $39, $99 or $199 one-time. Pro and up
    are featured.
- **Cadence:** weekly, Monday to Sunday. The free section is ranked by upvotes alone,
  and free launches have won whole weeks. About 37% of submissions went live in week 42.
- **Fields:**
  - name, tagline, description, URL.
  - ≤3 categories.
  - logo ≤2 MB and up to 5 screenshots ≤10 MB, as PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF (no SVG).
  - pricing, an optional discount, a first comment, and optional FAQs and alternatives.
- **Link:** dofollow once the badge check passes.
- **Do:** run its launch-readiness audit before submitting, because most submissions are
  sent back.
- **Sources:** nicklaunches.com/llms.txt, /submit/, /terms and /api/v1/openapi.json.
